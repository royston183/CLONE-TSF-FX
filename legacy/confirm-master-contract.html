Note: This queue only ever shows Master Contracts still awaiting MO action — Release to MO or Error Correction. Both are treated the same way: select one or more using the checkboxes, then use Confirm/Reject Selected above to act on all of them at once. Once a contract is Confirmed or Rejected it leaves this list entirely, which is also why Deal ID is always blank here - a Deal ID can only be assigned after Confirmation. Click any MC Number to open its child-contract breakdown; from there you can also confirm or reject a single contract. Click a column heading to sort.
